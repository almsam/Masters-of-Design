## Approved Master of Design Elective Courses:


### Winter Term 1 (September – December 2026)

#### CCS_O 506 M.F.A. Graduate Colloquium
> Multi-disciplinary seminar dealing with various approaches and issues in contemporary creative research methods as relating to the disciplines of Visual Arts, Media Arts, Creative Writing, Performance, and Curation. Restricted to students in the M.F.A. program. Restricted to students in the M.F.A program or permission of the Department of Creative Studies.
Class times: Thursday 10am - 1pm
Instructor: https://fccs.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact/matthew-rader/
Max class size: 15

#### IMTC_O 505 - Fundamentals of Immersive Technologies PDHC_O 501 - Product Design for Human Comfort
> Immersive technology principles; design of AR/MR/VR platforms; immersive interaction techniques; 3D user interfaces; custom XR app design; applications to mobile and wearable devices.
Class times: Thursday 11am - 2pm
Instructor: https://cmps.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact/pourang-irani/
Max class size: 20

#### IMTC_O 506 - User-Centered Immersive Design ENGR_O 598 – Student-led individual course experience
> Immersive design; user-centered and customer-oriented design; project-based learning; project conceptualization; industry- and community-sourced applications of immersive technologies.
Class times: Wednesday 11am - 2pm
Instructor: https://engineering.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact/abbas-milani/
Max class size: 30

#### PDHC_O 500 - Fundamentals of Human Comfort
> Human comfort perception; human physiological reactions; subjective perception of comfort and human psychological response; comfort attributes of clothing and wearable devices; interaction of environmental stimuli with humans; comfort enhancement methods.
Class times: Friday 8am - 11am
Instructor: unknown at time of writing
Max class size: 30

#### ENGR_O 598 – Student-led individual course experience (SLICE)1
> A personalized, student-designed learning experience that allows MDes students to pursue a focused area of
growth aligned with their goals, interests, and emerging practice. Students work with faculty mentors and
external partners to define learning outcomes, activities, and deliverables, creating a tailored educational
experience that complements the cohort journey and expands beyond traditional course structures.


### Winter Term 2 (January – April 2027)

#### IMTC_O 507 - Immersive Technology Design Studio
> Application of immersive technologies design skills; interactive immersive technology production; reflection on practice and critical thinking; art in contemporary XR production. Prerequisite: All of IMTC 505, IMTC 506.
Class times: Tuesday 2:30pm - 5:30pm
Instructor: https://gradstudies.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact/aleksandra-dulic/
Max class size: 15

#### PDHC_O 501 - Product Design for Human Comfort
> The course focuses on user-centred design principles to enhance physical, physiological, and psychological aspects of human comfort. Includes project-based learning, collaboration with industry and community partners, market analysis, and transdisciplinary design methods. Emphasizes equity, diversity, and inclusion (EDI) for accessible and inclusive solutions. Prerequisite: PDHC_O 500.
Class times: Wednesday 11am - 2pm
Instructor: https://engineering.ok.ubc.ca/about/contact/abbas-milani/
Max class size: 30
