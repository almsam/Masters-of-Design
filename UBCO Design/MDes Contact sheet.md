### Political leadership:

**President: Dr. Benoit-Antoine Bacon**
Dr. Bacon is the UBC Vancouver based leader of both UBC Campuses, holding role as Vancouver Principal and Vice Chancellor to the UBC Vancouver Senate. The President’s office is directly accountable only to the Senate, Board of Governors, and university act.
- presidents.office@ubc.ca

**Okanagan Principal: Dr. Lesley Cormack**
As the principal of UBC’s Okanagan campus specifically, Dr. Cormack oversees UBCO’s academic and non academic operations at a high level. Dr. Cormack also holds position as the Deputy Vice-Chancellor to the Okanagan Senate. The principals office is accountable to the Okanagan Senate and Board of Governors in addition to the president’s office
- ubco.principal@ubc.ca

**Okanagan Provost: Dr. Marie Tarrant**
The Provost & Vice-President Academic is responsible for all things academic - including overseeing the faculties, schools, collages, and departments - as well as the library and a few other departments
- ubco.provost@ubc.ca

**Associate Vice president of Finance & Operations: Robert Einarson**
The AVP FO is the peak of the non academic branch, overseeing everything from residence, food, parking, childcare, security, first aid, and building management
- finance-operations.ok@ubc.ca

**Associate Vice President of Students: Dr. Dale Mullings**
The AVP Students deals in all things students. While the academic and non academic branches keep the ship afloat, the students team ensures life onboard can thrive. The students department oversees things including mental health services & accessibility at a high level, as well as athletics & recreation, global engagement, and even supplemental educational resources
- avps.ok@ubc.ca

### Institutions of note:

**The Students Union:**
Independent from UBC’s Academic and Executive operations, the SUO is an independent group meant to represent students themselves. These guys are known for clubs, food services, events, and more
- https://www.suo.ca/contact-directory/


**The Library:**
Here you will find numerous departments such as the Student Learning Hub, Makerspace, and more.
- libquestions.ok@ubc.ca

**Student experience office:**
This lot work a lot with minority groups such as with the black students program and pride programming, and also contribute to student learning via Community Service Learning and the Student Learning Hub
- studentexperience.ok@ubc.ca

**Campus planning office:**
These folks are split into 3 offices
 -- Campus Planning - who focus on outdoor spaces and developments
 -- Sustainability Planning - who focus on long term planning of UBCO
 -- & Sustainable Transportation - who work in consultation with Kelowna Transit & parking services to ensure students and staff can get to and from campus
- transportation.ok@ubc.ca


**Campus Operations and Risk Management:**
This department is a peak example of an executive branch, they over see campus security who act as UBCO’s police, fire, and first aid; the Health & Safety department; Energy Services; Facilities Management; Salto & Key services; Waste management; and a bunch more
- https://operations.ok.ubc.ca/
